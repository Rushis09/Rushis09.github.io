About

§ 01

I spent four years inside two of India's trading platforms — first at Angel One, then SBI Securities — not as an engineer, but as the person customers called when something broke. That put me in the room for beta validation, defect triage, and CRM-driven issue resolution long before I had a QA title.

In 2025 I started treating that instinct as a discipline: reading up on structured testing, then building QA Book — a full-stack QA management platform — to give myself a real system to test against. I'm now formalizing that into test plans, test cases, and an automation practice with Playwright and Python.
