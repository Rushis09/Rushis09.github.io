01 / ABOUT

My QA journey started close to the customer. At Angel One and SBI Cap Securities, I worked around trading-platform workflows, beta validation, issue reproduction, fix verification, and regression checks.

That experience taught me to look beyond whether a screen works: I think about user journeys, financial workflows, edge cases, and the impact of a defect. I am now developing my technical QA skills through Tricentis Tosca, Playwright with TypeScript, API checks in Swagger, and a product I can test end to end.

My goal: bring domain understanding, structured testing, and growing automation skills to a QA team where I can keep learning and contribute to dependable releases.
